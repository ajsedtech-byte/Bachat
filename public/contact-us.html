Which email should you use?

For shopper account, order, delivery, payment, return, or refund support, use bachat@seekhen.com. For seller onboarding, KYC, pricing, dashboard, catalog, and shop operations, use bachat@seekhen.com. For privacy or data rights questions, use bachat@seekhen.com. For local business partnerships, category campaigns, sponsorships, and city-level marketplace collaboration, use support@seekhen.com.

Good contact messages are short but complete. They explain who you are, what happened, which order or account is affected, and what outcome you need. This lets the team act without repeatedly asking for basic details.

Official contact addresses

Shoppers should write to bachat@seekhen.com for account, order, payment, delivery, return, and refund help. Sellers should also write to bachat@seekhen.com for onboarding, KYC, pricing, dashboard, catalog, and active-order support. Privacy requests should be sent to bachat@seekhen.com so the account and data request can be verified through the same Bachat support route. Partnership requests should be sent to support@seekhen.com with the proposal, city, category, and contact details.

What to include in the first message

Identity and role

Registered phone or email, city, and whether you are a shopper, seller, delivery partner, or admin user.

Reference details

Order ID, payment reference, seller name, shop name, request ID, or dashboard page where the issue happened.

Issue and resolution

A concise explanation plus whether you need refund review, delivery help, account access, cancellation, or seller setup guidance.
