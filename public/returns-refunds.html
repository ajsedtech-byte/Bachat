Returns and refunds

Resolve order issues fairly.

Returns and refunds need careful review because local orders can involve different sellers, product categories, payment states, and delivery confirmations. Bachat support uses order evidence and seller policy to choose the most reasonable resolution path.

Report an issueHelp center
Keep order details ready.

Photos, order ID, seller name, payment status, and delivery date make return and refund checks faster and fairer.

When a case may qualify

A return or refund review may be appropriate when the item is wrong, damaged, missing, not delivered, duplicated in payment, cancelled by the seller, or significantly different from what was confirmed. Some categories may have stricter rules, especially perishable products, opened sealed items, custom orders, or products where condition changes quickly after delivery.

The buyer should report the issue as soon as possible and avoid using or altering the item further. Clear photos, packaging condition, delivery timing, and order references help support verify what happened.

How review usually works

Issue is reported

The shopper explains the problem with order ID, product details, photos, and requested resolution.

Support checks evidence

The team reviews order status, seller input, payment state, delivery confirmation, and product category restrictions.

Resolution is selected

Depending on the case, support may coordinate replacement, pickup, cancellation, partial refund, full refund, or seller follow-up.

Useful references

If the issue started during delivery or requires direct help, keep these references open while preparing your message.
