What belongs on the blog

The blog should not be only announcements. It should explain common workflows in plain language: why detailed product requests matter, how seller quotes should be compared, how delivery updates work, what information is needed for refund review, and how sellers can reduce cancellations.

Because Bachat connects multiple roles, every good article should make one workflow easier to understand for a real person. A shopper guide should reduce confusion before purchase. A seller guide should improve daily execution. A release note should explain what changed and why it matters.

Editorial structure

Shopper education

Guides about product requests, comparing quotes, choosing sellers, tracking delivery, and contacting support.

Seller education

Playbooks about onboarding, quote quality, catalog hygiene, pricing clarity, delivery readiness, and customer trust.

Platform updates

Notes about dashboard improvements, support changes, payment flows, delivery tracking, KYC updates, and city launches.
