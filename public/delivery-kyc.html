Verify your identity

We only store the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar number — never the full 12-digit UID on this server. Government-grade UIDAI “eKYC” (OTP/XML) is not implemented here; use DigiLocker (below) plus counsel-approved policy, or integrate a licensed ASP. See docs/DELIVERY-KYC.md in the repository for paths and compliance notes.

DigiLocker (optional)

Going live requires registering as a DigiLocker / Meri Pehchaan partner (e.g. partner portal), HTTPS redirect URIs, and portal review — not just .env keys.

After login we sync metadata for issued documents (titles, issuers). Full PDF/XML download stays off on the server unless your legal team enables it. This is not a substitute for UIDAI eKYC or your counsel-approved KYC policy — you still complete last-4 Aadhaar below unless ops changes that.

Legal & data use (DigiLocker + this form)
  • Purpose limitation: Bachat uses identity signals only to onboard and audit delivery partners, unless we publish a different notice and consent.
  • Not legal advice: Whether DigiLocker metadata or last-4 Aadhaar satisfies KYC law for your entity must be confirmed by qualified counsel.
  • Retention: Document URIs and metadata are stored server-side for your account to support optional downloads and audits; define your own retention and deletion policy before production.
  • Sensitive payloads: Some issued PDFs or XML certificates can include masked Aadhaar or PAN — treat downloads as highly confidential and disable server file fetch until policies and DIGILOCKER_ALLOW_FILE_FETCH are approved.
