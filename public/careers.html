The kind of work we do

Bachat work lives at the intersection of technology and on-ground commerce. A support issue may reveal a product problem. A seller onboarding delay may reveal a process gap. A failed delivery may reveal that address capture needs improvement. People who do well here are curious enough to understand the whole chain instead of only fixing the visible symptom.

Roles may include operations, product support, seller onboarding, customer support, city coordination, catalog improvement, quality review, and technical delivery. Across all roles, clear communication and ownership matter more than fancy language.

How we approach problems

Listen before solving

We look at real shopper, seller, and support behavior before changing a process. The first answer is not always the right answer.

Turn repeated confusion into systems

If the same issue appears again and again, the better solution is usually a clearer flow, better copy, stronger validation, or improved dashboard visibility.

Measure usefulness

A change is valuable when orders complete more smoothly, sellers respond with less confusion, and support has better context.

How to apply

Email careers@seekhen.com with your resume or profile link, city, preferred role area, and a short note about one local commerce problem you think should be easier. That note helps us understand how you observe real-world systems.
