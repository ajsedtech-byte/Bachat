The kind of work we do

Bachat work lives at the intersection of technology, local commerce, and on-ground execution. The team works with real shoppers, shopkeepers, delivery partners, and city operators, so every role is connected to practical outcomes: clearer seller onboarding, faster customer support, better catalog quality, smoother ordering, and stronger marketplace trust.

We look for people who can understand the full chain of a problem, communicate clearly, and turn repeated issues into simple operating systems. You do not need fancy language to make an impact here. You need ownership, consistency, and the patience to solve details that matter to real users.

01

Marketplace operations

Coordinate daily marketplace activity across sellers, customer requests, orders, and support cases. This includes tracking gaps, improving response speed, and helping the team keep work organized as volume grows.

02

Seller onboarding and success

Help local shopkeepers complete onboarding, understand dashboard flows, improve product information, and respond to customer demand with clarity. Strong follow-up and patient communication matter here.

03

Customer support and trust

Resolve shopper questions around requests, quotes, payments, delivery, returns, and account issues. The goal is to give customers accurate answers while identifying patterns that the product or operations team should fix.

04

Catalog and quality review

Improve product listings, category structure, pricing clarity, and marketplace quality. This work helps shoppers compare options more easily and helps sellers present their inventory professionally.

05

City coordination

Support city-level execution by understanding local areas, seller clusters, delivery availability, and recurring customer needs. Good city coordination turns local knowledge into better service.

06

Product and technical delivery

Work with product flows, dashboards, data, automation, and technical improvements that make operations easier. The best product ideas often come from observing repeated support or seller problems closely.

How we approach the work

Listen before solving

We study real shopper, seller, and support behavior before changing a process. The visible complaint is often only one part of the actual problem.

Turn confusion into systems

If the same issue appears repeatedly, we improve the flow, copy, validation, dashboard visibility, checklist, or operating process so the issue becomes less likely next time.

Measure usefulness

A change is valuable when orders move more smoothly, sellers respond with less confusion, customers get better answers, and support has stronger context.

Communicate with ownership

We prefer direct, complete updates: what happened, what is blocked, what was tried, and what needs to happen next.

How to apply

You can apply directly through the form above, or email careers@seekhen.com with your resume or profile link. Please include your city, preferred role area, current availability, and a short note about one local commerce problem you think should be easier.

A strong application is practical and specific. We are interested in how you observe real-world systems, how clearly you communicate, and whether you can connect a customer or seller problem to a better process, product flow, or operating habit.

Include

Your basic details

Share your full name, email, phone number if you want to include it, city or work area, and the type of role you are interested in: operations, seller support, customer care, city coordination, catalog quality, product support, or technical delivery.

Attach

Resume or profile

Upload a PDF, DOC, or DOCX resume through the form, or email a resume/profile link. Keep it focused on relevant experience, responsibilities handled, tools used, languages known, and examples of ownership.

Explain

Your problem-solving note

Write a short note about one local commerce problem you have noticed. For example: seller onboarding confusion, late customer replies, unclear pricing, delivery coordination, return handling, catalog quality, or support follow-up.

Show

How you think

Tell us what you observed, why it matters, who is affected, and what simple improvement you would try first. The goal is not a perfect answer; it is to understand your judgment and attention to practical details.

What happens after you apply

  1. Application review: We review your details, role preference, city, experience, and resume against current team needs.
  2. Shortlisting: If there is a match, the team contacts you for more context about your background, availability, and preferred work area.
  3. Practical conversation: We may discuss a real Bachat-style situation to understand how you communicate, prioritize, and solve problems.
  4. Next steps: Selected applicants receive the role details, expected responsibilities, work mode, and joining process.
