Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Bachat ("we", "our", "us") operates a local commerce platform for buyers, sellers, delivery partners, and team users. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, role, city/region.
  • Order and transaction data: requests, quotes, orders, payments, disputes.
  • Location data (with consent): address text, GPS coordinates, and delivery route points.
  • Device and usage data required for app security, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide marketplace features (request, quote, order, delivery tracking).
  • Authenticate users and secure accounts.
  • Enable customer support and issue resolution.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Location Data

We collect precise location only when you explicitly confirm or share location in app flows (for example, saving address or delivery tracking). You can stop sharing by disabling location permission in your device settings.

4. Data Sharing

We share only what is necessary with relevant parties in the transaction flow (buyer, seller, delivery partner, admin/ops) and service providers used for app operations (for example, hosting, email, and payment processing).

5. Data Retention

We retain data as needed for service operation, legal compliance, dispute handling, and security. Delivery route/location history is retained for limited periods based on system policy.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational controls to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal/business requirements.

8. Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact us at: privacy@seekhen.com
