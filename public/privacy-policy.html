Bachat Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 6, 2026

Bachat helps buyers discover local prices from sellers and complete purchases with delivery tracking. This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data across buyer, seller, delivery, and team/admin experiences.

1) Data We Collect

  • Profile data: name, email, phone, city/region, account role.
  • Commerce data: requests, quotes, orders, payment status, disputes, and support records.
  • Location data (consent-based): address text, pincode, latitude/longitude, delivery route points.
  • Technical/security data: device/browser details, session and authentication logs.

2) Why We Use Data

  • To run the Bachat marketplace and complete request-to-order workflows.
  • To provide delivery and route tracking features for relevant parties.
  • To protect accounts, prevent abuse/fraud, and maintain platform safety.
  • To meet legal, tax, regulatory, and grievance-handling obligations.

3) Location and Delivery Tracking

Precise location is collected only when you explicitly provide it in app flows (for example, saving address, confirming map pin, or delivery tracking). Location visibility is role-limited (buyer/seller/delivery/admin as required for order fulfillment). You can control location permission in device settings.

4) Sharing of Data

We share data only as necessary to operate the service:

  • Between transaction participants (buyer, seller, delivery partner) when needed.
  • With trusted service providers (hosting, notifications/email, payment processors).
  • With authorities where legally required.

5) Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as needed for operations, legal compliance, dispute handling, and security. Delivery route history is retained for a limited period per system policy.

6) Security Practices

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards (access controls, authentication checks, and secure infrastructure practices). No system is 100% risk-free, but we continuously improve controls.

7) Your Choices and Rights

  • Update your profile information in the app.
  • Request correction/deletion subject to business and legal requirements.
  • Withdraw location permission from device settings at any time.

8) Children's Privacy

Bachat is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to consent independently.

9) Contact and Grievance

For privacy questions or requests, contact: privacy@seekhen.com

10) Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
