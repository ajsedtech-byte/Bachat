Small habits, better orders.

Fast responses, accurate prices, clear stock status, and timely order updates are the foundation of repeat trust.

Before a seller starts accepting orders

The shop profile should be complete enough for buyers and support to understand the business. This includes shop name, category, service area, address, owner contact, operating hours, delivery or pickup preference, and any KYC or payment details required for activation. Incomplete setup often creates avoidable support issues later.

Catalog quality also matters. Product names should be understandable, prices should be current, stock should not be misleading, and substitutions should be communicated before an order is confirmed. A seller who quotes clearly from the beginning will usually face fewer disputes later.

Order quality checklist

Before quoting

Check stock, variant, price, delivery feasibility, expected preparation time, and whether the buyer requested a specific brand or model.

After accepting

Pack correctly, update readiness, confirm substitutions where needed, and coordinate pickup or delivery handoff without delay.

After completion

Review cancellations, failed deliveries, support cases, and repeated stock issues so future quotes become more accurate.
