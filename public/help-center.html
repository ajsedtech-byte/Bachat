Good details make support faster.

The best support request includes role, account contact, order reference, current status, and the exact resolution needed.

Account and access help

If you cannot sign in, first confirm that you are using the correct phone or email and the correct role. A shopper account, seller account, delivery account, and admin account may have different entry points. When contacting support, explain which page you used, what message appeared, and whether you recently changed phone, password, or role.

Order, payment, and delivery help

Order support works best when the message includes an order ID, seller name, payment status, delivery status, and a short explanation of what changed. For example, a delayed delivery, wrong item, duplicate payment, missing update, or cancellation request each needs a different path. Support can move faster when the first message already identifies the issue type.

Identify the role

Tell support if you are the buyer, seller, delivery partner, or admin user.

Share the reference

Use order ID, payment ID, shop name, request ID, or registered phone number.

State the expected outcome

Say whether you need tracking, cancellation, refund review, account unlock, or seller onboarding help.

Safety guidance

Bachat support will not ask for your password, full card details, OTP, or unrelated payment credentials. If a message asks for sensitive information that does not belong in a support conversation, do not share it. Use official support channels and keep screenshots of suspicious requests.
