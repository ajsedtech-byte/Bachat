Clear status beats guesswork.

Delivery clarity depends on accurate seller updates, complete customer addresses, and timely support when something changes.

How delivery availability is decided

Delivery availability can depend on pincode, seller service area, order size, product type, and whether a delivery partner or seller-managed delivery option is available. Some shops may support delivery for nearby customers but require pickup for bulky, fragile, urgent, or restricted products. The final quote or order confirmation should be reviewed carefully before assuming delivery is included.

A complete address also matters. Shoppers should provide building name, street, landmark, pincode, contact number, and map location where available. Missing address details can create failed delivery attempts even when the seller and delivery partner are ready.

Typical delivery status flow

Accepted by seller

The seller confirms that the order can be fulfilled and begins preparing the items.

Packed or ready

The shop marks the order ready for pickup, dispatch, or handoff after checking stock and packaging.

Out for delivery

The order is moving toward the customer, and delays should be reported with the order reference.

Delivered or unresolved

The order closes after delivery, or support helps review failed delivery, wrong address, or item issues.
