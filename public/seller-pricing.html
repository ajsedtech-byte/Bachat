Pricing

Seller pricing should match real shop needs.

Bachat pricing is best understood as a conversation about marketplace access, local visibility, support effort, operational complexity, and order volume. The right seller plan depends on how the shop wants to use the platform and what level of help it needs to succeed.

Know the cost before scaling.

A plan should be clear about access, visibility, support, activation, and any commercial terms before a seller commits.

What affects seller pricing

Pricing may depend on the seller category, service area, number of products, expected order volume, onboarding effort, promotional visibility, support needs, and whether special delivery or campaign coordination is required. A small shop that only needs basic quote and order handling may need a different plan from a multi-category seller that wants broader visibility and operational support.

Before choosing a plan, sellers should prepare shop documents, category details, service areas, expected order count, delivery preference, and the kind of support they expect from Bachat. This makes the pricing discussion concrete instead of vague.

How to evaluate a plan

Start with the workflow

Understand whether the shop mainly needs discovery, quote management, order tracking, catalog support, or promotional visibility.

Check the support level

New sellers may need onboarding and KYC help, while active sellers may need reporting, catalog cleanup, or campaign guidance.

Confirm final terms

Review fees, included services, payment flow, cancellation rules, support commitments, and promotional scope before activation.
